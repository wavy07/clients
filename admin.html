🏷 Banners & coupons

Banners — branding, promos & coupons

Make as many as you like. Each one can have your logo / picture, your own colours, a coupon code (customers tap to copy it), a button that opens any page or link, a start/end date, and can be shown only on the pages you pick. Top bar = slim strip above the menu · Big card = large card at the top of the page · Hanging tag = small tag at the top-right · Popup = centre of the screen · Slider = swipeable rotating banners · Floating card = corner card · Bottom bar = sticks to the bottom. Pick a look (glass, neon, outline…), an effect (pulse, shine, scrolling text), who sees it, and a live countdown.
